The starting point

What is cryptographic discovery?

Cryptographic discovery identifies where and how cryptography is used in software, systems and services.

Editorial synthesis based on NCCoE’s migration project.

  1. 01 / DISCOVERY

    Observe

    Identify cryptographic assets and their use within the scope of an observation.

  2. 02 / INVENTORY

    Organise

    Bring findings together with context and relationships. Keep the observation scope and date.

  3. 03 / CBOM

    Represent

    Describe cryptographic assets and dependencies in a machine-readable model, such as CycloneDX CBOM.

Discovery → inventory → CBOM is an editorial explanation of the relationship, not a prescribed process. A CBOM does not by itself establish complete discovery coverage. Sources: NCCoE · CycloneDX CBOM guide.

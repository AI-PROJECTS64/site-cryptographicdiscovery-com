Terminology

In practice, “cryptographic discovery” is closely connected to cryptographic inventory and crypto-agility. The category covers the disciplined identification of cryptography where it actually lives (applications, endpoints, networks, certificates, dependencies, cloud environments and appliances), so that organizations can plan migrations (including post-quantum) with traceability and governance.
